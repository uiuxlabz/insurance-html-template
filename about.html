Our Story

From a Small Office to a National Provider

LifeSure was founded in 1999 with a simple mission: to make quality insurance accessible to every family. What started as a two-person operation in a modest office has grown into a nationally recognized provider serving over 50,000 clients.

Our founders, driven by firsthand experience with the complexities of insurance claims, envisioned a company that would put people first. Today, that vision continues to guide every policy we write and every claim we process.

We believe insurance should be straightforward, fairly priced, and backed by people who genuinely care. That philosophy has earned us an A+ rating and the trust of families across the country.
